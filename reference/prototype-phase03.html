HOW TO PLAY

  • Zone 1 (top-left): Energy, Score, Level/EXP
  • Zone 2 (top-right): Pause button
  • Zone 3 (bottom-left): Movement — 4 columns
  •   ⬅️ Left · ↖️ Left (diagonal, reserved) · ↗️ Right (diagonal, reserved) · ➡️ Right
  •   Drag your touch DOWN past a small deadzone = Slide
  •   Quick tap while sliding = Cancel
  • Zone 4 (bottom-right): Actions
  •   ⏹️ (left half) = Jump · again mid-air = Air Dash
  •   ⏺️ (right half) = Shoot · Hold = Charge shot
  • WEAPON button (bottom-center) = pause & open Weapon Select
  •   Tap a weapon in the grid to equip it (also recolors your suit)
  •   Keyboard: TAB toggles Weapon Select · Q/W cycle
  • Tap a weapon icon (top HUD) to select it directly
  • Each weapon levels 1→10 separately; levelling up ranks up your equipped weapon
  • All weapons are tuned to the same damage-per-second at Lv 1
  • Keyboard: Arrows/AD move, Space jump, hold Z to charge, X slide, Q/W cycle
  • Walking left of screen-center never scrolls the camera back
  • A glowing BOSS DOOR appears every 60s — walk in to fight
  • 17 robot masters, one per element — none repeat until all are seen
  • Beating a boss makes it return at a higher LAYER (1→2→3) in future runs
  • Pits and spikes are instant death — jump/dash over them
  • EXP (this run only) comes from distance + kills — level up for in-run bonuses
  • CHIPS (persistent) are earned per run — spend in the HUB on permanent Upgrades
  • Kill combos multiply score
  • E-Tanks = full heal
