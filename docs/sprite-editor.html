A STEP is one 1/60s slice of the game's fixed simulation. On a 60Hz screen one step is one refresh, so "5 steps" and "5 screen frames" are the same thing there. On a 120Hz phone the drawn frame lasts the same time, it just spans 10 refreshes — so steps are the number that means something and refreshes are not.

HOLD is a number the game always had; it was just spelled fps. The player sheet is fps: 12, so every frame is held 1/12 s = 5 steps — which is why 5 is the default. idle overrode it with animFps: 1.5, so its frames hold for 40 steps. One number for a whole sheet; now one per frame.

IDLE — 2 frames, 40 steps each (0.67s), full cycle 1.33s. A breath, not a cycle: at the run's 12fps a two-frame idle blinks six times a second and reads as a strobe.
RUN — 6 frames, 5 steps each (0.083s), full stride 30 steps (0.5s), carrying him 41px. JUMP — three one-frame poses picked from velocity, never cycled. SLIDE — held for all 26 steps (0.43s).
