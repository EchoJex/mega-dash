# Rooms

The six boss rooms drawn in 16-bit, captured from the game itself at twice its size (960 × 448 for a 480 × 224 screen): Proto Mk0's bunker, Blaze Man's volcano, Tempest Man's storm, Volt Man's power room, Thorn Man's greenhouse and Strike Man's fight pit. Shown at layer 2, except Thorn Man's at layer 1; the HUD and thumb pads are in the pictures as they were on 6 Oct 2026 (the pads have since become see-through, with a thin gold border).

- **Use them as the reference for any new room**: the shading, the dithering, how busy a room can be while the white player still reads.
- The bosses in them are still plain rectangles; their sprites have not been drawn.
- Drawn by Claude in Claude Design at the owner's request and approved by the owner on 4 Oct 2026. The rooms' code is in `src/systems/arena-art/`, one file per room.
- Each room is code, not a picture: these screenshots are for looking at, never for putting into the game.
- Wraith Man's derelict chapel (drawn 23 Oct 2026, `src/systems/arena-art/wraith.js`) is in the game but has no picture here yet.
