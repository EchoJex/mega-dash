# Sprites

`player.png` is the game's own player sheet: twenty-two 24 × 24 frames in a 528 × 24 strip, twelve colours, the example every new sprite follows. Show it enlarged by a whole number with pixelated scaling, never smoothed.

- Frames 1–6 idle, 7–14 run, 15–17 the jump poses (rising, top, falling), 18–22 slide.
- Drawn by Claude at the owner's request on 29 Sep 2026. Its source is `design/sprites/player.sprite`.

`boss-strike-body.png` is Strike Man's body (Fighting): a rusty robot boxer on a tank-tread base, every clip including both stagger stages. Drawn by Claude in Claude Design at the owner's request, 31 Oct 2026, restyled on treads 2 Nov 2026. Source `design/sprites/boss-strike-body.sprite`.

`boss-thorn-body.png` is Thorn Man's body (Grass): a leaf-horned ninja with vine-spring arms ending in shuriken, every clip; the owner picked each part from options on a design canvas. Drawn by Claude in Claude Design at the owner's request, 4 Nov 2026. Source `design/sprites/boss-thorn-body.sprite`.

`spiglet.png` is SPIGLET, the ground minion: four 16 × 16 frames. Each boss's copy is baked from it with his primary. Source `design/sprites/spiglet.sprite`.
