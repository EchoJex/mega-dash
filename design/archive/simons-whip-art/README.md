# Simon's Whip Art — the Grass weapon

A Claude Design canvas, 22–23 Oct 2026. Link while it lived: https://claude.ai/artifact/E4yaHdizD1ixQ5JUxGdadV

**In the game:** `design/sprites/weapon-simons_whip-{held,body}.sprite` — the thorny vine in every
crack and his three Simon poses. The coiled whip at rest is not from here: the owner drew it pixel
by pixel, starting from the editable idle frame on `IdleWhip`.

What the threads settled: no crouching anywhere in the game; whipping during a slide keeps his
slide legs, both feet leading, with the standing upper body on top ("lowcrack" renamed
"slidingcrack"); a narrower stance that keeps his centre point; a lower, droopier overhead swing;
and the rest of the vine keeps medium damage, as the tracker says (option B). `Stills` shows every
hit area on all 25 frames, drawn for that review.

`assets/` holds the 45 pictures the pages showed.
The owner's words: `owner-words.md`. AI-made: drawn by Claude (Claude Design) at the owner's
request, except the coiled whip at rest.
