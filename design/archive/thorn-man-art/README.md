# Thorn Man Art — the Grass boss

A Claude Design canvas, 4 Nov 2026. Link while it lived: https://claude.ai/artifact/KwKoHyEzw7Kusfz69KD4LC

**In the game:** his body, `design/sprites/boss-thorn-body.sprite` — a leaf-horned ninja in leaf
armour, idle look 04 with small brown tool pouches strapped to his thigh, and stiff coiled
vine-spring arms (look 05, fewer turns) ending in shuriken. His single eye is one glowing slit in
his hood. This canvas is also where the owner set the rule that every boss has a single eye.

**Picked but not in the game yet** (no sheet for them): tile #3 and leaf kunai #4.

**Picked later: seed pod B, the smaller egg** (owner, 11 Nov 2026: "Seed pod B"). Not in the game
yet, like the tile and the kunai. How it got there: the seed pod. The owner asked for "the original bulb, but with the low movement
leafy base", Claude asked which of two bulbs that meant (A, the round ribbed first bulb; B, the
smaller egg), and the last word is "show me these two side by side". That row is on `pages/Props.dc.html`.

Pages: `Main` (the board), `Clips` (his clips at game speed), `Props` (tile, kunai, seed pod).
