TEMPEST MAN · BATCH 3 · 6 DIVEBOMBS

PICK A DIVEBOMB, 1 TO 6. LAYER 3 ONLY: HIS TANK IS EMPTY, SO HE HANGS IN THE NEAREST CORNER FOR 1.5 SECONDS WATCHING THE PLAYER (HIS TANK BAR FLASHING THE RED X), THEN DIVES ONTO WHERE THE PLAYER WAS AND THROWS UP A WAVE EACH WAY, THREE TIMES HIS HEIGHT.

PLAYED AT GAME SPEED, 60 STEPS A SECOND: THE WAIT (TWO PICTURES, 45 STEPS EACH), THE DIVE (TWO PICTURES LOOPING WHILE HE FALLS AT THE GAME'S SPEED), THE LANDING (TWO PICTURES), THEN THE WAVES. THESE ARE ROUGH: ENOUGH TO JUDGE THE ACTING, NOT FINAL PIXELS. THE WAVES ARE THE GAME'S PLAIN STAND-IN, 108 PIXELS TALL, AND GET THEIR OWN VARIANTS LATER AS ARENA FURNITURE. ONLY 6 USES HIS JETS IN THE DIVE; IN THE OTHER FIVE THEY ARE OFF. THE BLUE BOXES ARE THE HIT BOXES, THE GOLD PIXEL WHERE THE GAME HOLDS HIM. NOTHING HERE IS IN THE GAME YET.
