TEMPEST MAN · IDLE · 01 DIVER AND 16 BALL JOINTS

THE PROSE, TRIMMED TO WHAT 16 SHOWS: A HULK SEALED IN A BRASS PRESSURE SUIT BUILT FOR THE SEA FLOOR. HE IS ALL TORSO, WITH NO NECK, ON SHORT LEGS. HIS ARMS ARE CHAINS OF BALL JOINTS, AND ONE ENDS IN A DEAD, RUSTED MINE. NO FACE SHOWS, ONLY ONE WIDE EYE BEHIND GLASS, LOOKING AT YOU: THE BLACK DOT IS ITS PUPIL AND THE GLOWING TEAL AROUND IT IS ITS SCLERA.

THE TWO KEPT LOOKS, BESIDE THE PLAYER AT TRUE SCALE ON HIS ROOM'S DARK BLUE. 16 IS ABOUT 35 WIDE AND 44 TALL; THE BLUE BOX IS STILL HIS 27 × 36 HIT BOX, WHICH ONLY THE CODE CAN CHANGE. THE GOLD PIXEL IS WHERE THE GAME HOLDS HIM. THE BOB IS A STAND-IN, NOT TO BE BUILT. EVERY OTHER LOOK IS ON THE ARCHIVE PAGE. NOTHING HERE IS IN THE GAME YET.
