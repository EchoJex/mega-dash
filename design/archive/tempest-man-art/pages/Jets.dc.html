THE TANK BAR

THE PACK, 12 TIMES LIFE SIZE. THE BLUE BAR DRAINS FROM FULL TO EMPTY WHILE HE FLIES, THEN GOES BLACK WITH A THIN RED X: THE WARNING THAT THE SLAM IS COMING.

THE BAR NOW SITS IN A RECESS IN THE CASING: A SHADOWED LIP ABOVE AND BEHIND IT, A LIT LIP BELOW AND IN FRONT, ROUNDED CORNERS, AND THE WATER SHADED UNDER THE TOP LIP. THE BAR IS 9 × 5 PIXELS, TWO ROWS TALLER THAN ON THE LOOK YOU PICKED, BECAUSE AN X DOES NOT READ IN 3 ROWS. THE RED IS THE MINE'S LIGHT RUST, SO HIS SHEET STAYS AT 15 COLOURS.

THE TANK IS NOW ITS OWN PIECE, DRAWN BEHIND HIM AND SET 2 PIXELS FURTHER BACK, SO THE WHOLE BAR SHOWS PAST HIS SHOULDER WITH HIS OUTLINE CLEAR OF IT. THE BAR IS PART OF THE TANK'S PICTURES. THE SPEED HERE IS A STAND-IN, NOT TO BE BUILT: 9 STEPS OF DRAIN AT 10 STEPS EACH, THEN THE X FOR 30 STEPS (THE LENGTH OF TODAY'S WARNING). IN THE GAME THE DRAIN WOULD FOLLOW HOW LONG HE REALLY FLIES, SO THE TANK NEEDS 11 PICTURES (10 FILL LEVELS AND THE X) ON A SHEET OF ITS OWN, DRAWN BEHIND HIS BODY. THAT IS A REQUEST FOR CLAUDE CODE.
