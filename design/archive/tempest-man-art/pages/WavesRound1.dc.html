ARCHIVE · TEMPEST MAN · TSUNAMI WAVES, ROUND 1 (BEFORE THE WAVE PASSED THROUGH THE WALL)

PICK A WAVE, 1 TO 6. LAYER 3: THE DIVEBOMB THROWS UP ONE OF THESE EACH WAY, 108 PIXELS TALL (THREE TIMES HIS HIT BOX). EACH CROSSES THE ROOM, BREAKS ON THE WALL WITH A BURST OF SPRAY, AND SINKS AWAY OVER 150 STEPS. THE PLAYER MUST JUMP UP THROUGH IT AND SKIP ALONG ITS TOP.

THE PLAYER HAS TO STAY VISIBLE INSIDE IT, SO EVERY WAVE IS SEE-THROUGH BELOW ITS CREST (EVERY OTHER PIXEL, THE WAY SNES GAMES FAKED SEE-THROUGH WATER), AND HE IS DRAWN IN FRONT OF IT. THE SPEED BUTTON PLAYS IT AT FULL, HALF OR QUARTER SPEED; STEP +1 PAUSES AND MOVES ON BY ONE STEP (1/60 S) EACH TAP. DRAWN IN PLAIN CODE TO JUDGE THE LOOK. THE BLUE BOX IS WHAT THE GAME COUNTS AS THE WAVE TODAY: ONLY 26 WIDE, AT ITS FRONT. EVERY WAVE HERE SLOPES AWAY BEHIND ITS FRONT, SO WHICHEVER YOU PICK, THE HANDOFF WILL ASK CLAUDE CODE TO MAKE THE BOX FOLLOW THE DRAWING. THE SPIKE BALLS HE DROPS AND THE ONES ON THE LAYER-3 WALLS ALREADY USE THE ROOM'S DRAWN SPIKE BALLS, SO THEY ARE NOT ON THIS BOARD. NOTHING HERE IS IN THE GAME YET.
