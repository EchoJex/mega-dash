# Granite Form — the Rock weapon

A Claude Design canvas, 26 Oct 2026. Link while it lived: https://claude.ai/artifact/QmkdtUVSGdkLewpK4YbP8U

**In the game:** `design/sprites/weapon-granite_form-{statue,boulder,rocker,stone}.sprite` — the
chest and monolith statues (the chest made a little smaller to match its hit box), the boulder,
the three-legged walker and the loose stone. The megaman statue is not from here: it is still the
placeholder, for the owner to draw.

The option boards keep every variant that was not picked: `BoulderOptions`, `WalkerOptions`,
`StubbyOptions` (the 24 idle variants, some with no middle leg segment) and `FormChangeDemo`.
`assets/` holds their twenty pictures and the 5x7 font.
The owner's words: `owner-words.md`. AI-made: drawn by Claude (Claude Design) at the owner's request.
