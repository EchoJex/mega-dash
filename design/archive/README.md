# The archive

Everything made along the way that did not end up in the game, kept for posterity: the variants
nobody picked, the ideas a canvas explored, the review pages that did their job. The owner's call
(10 Nov 2026): "I want to preserve the unused assets and ideas that have been generated along the
way for posterity."

## The rule

**When a canvas's art is in the game, the canvas is archived here and removed from Claude.** Claude
does it in the same piece of work that brings the art in, and asks before each removal, because a
removed artifact cannot be brought back.

## What goes in a folder

One folder per artifact, named after it (`thorn-man-art/`, `world-pieces/` ...):

- `README.md` — what it was, when, its link while it lived, what was picked from it, and the owner's
  words from its comment threads
- its pages, as published (`.html`)
- every picture it showed, as files beside the pages

Nothing here is loaded by the game or the tests. AI-made pictures keep the label they had.
