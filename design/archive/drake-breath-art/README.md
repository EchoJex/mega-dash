# Drake Breath Art — the Draco-Blaster (Dragon weapon)

A Claude Design canvas, 24 Oct 2026, from when the weapon was still called Drake Breath.
Link while it lived: https://claude.ai/artifact/N5KukMKFy3k5UHKYK3stzZ

**In the game:** `design/sprites/weapon-draco_blaster-{held,puff,wave,blast,beam,streak,body}.sprite`
— the dragon-head cannon in every clip, the breath puffs, the vapour-cone wave, the blast, the beam
and its streaks, and his wide, slightly crouched stance while it is busy. Things held in hand bob
with his idle, and `IdleCannon` is the editable idle frame the owner asked for ("as we did with the whip").

`pages/sprites/` keeps the six sheets as they stood on the canvas, under the old name, before they
were renamed in the repo. `assets/` holds the data files and the 5x7 font the pages used.
The owner's words: `owner-words.md`. AI-made: drawn by Claude (Claude Design) at the owner's request.
